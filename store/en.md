**Description** (paste as plain text)

Holdpad is a quick notes panel that opens beside any web page. Jot down a thought, save a passage you are reading, keep a link for later — without leaving the tab, without an account, and without anything leaving your computer.

It is a notepad for the things you need right now: the phone number from a support chat, three quotes for an essay, the error message you are about to search for, the shopping list you will want in an hour. Keep what matters with a pin, and clear the rest when the task is done.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Write quick notes on any page — press Alt+Shift+N or click the toolbar icon, type, and press Ctrl/Cmd+Enter.
• Save selected text from any website — select, right-click, "Save selection to Holdpad". Links, bold, lists and code blocks are kept, along with the page it came from.
• Save links and whole pages — right-click a link or a page to keep it with its title.
• Rich text notepad — headings, bullet and numbered lists, checklists, quotes, links and code blocks. Markdown shortcuts work as you type: "- " for a list, "# " for a heading.
• Search everything instantly — Ctrl/Cmd+K searches note text, source pages and tags, with matches highlighted.
• Pin and tag — pin what must stay; add one tag to group notes and click it to filter.
• List view or card view — scan titles fast, or read notes in full.
• Clear without fear — clear one note or every unpinned note at once. Undo is right there, and cleared notes wait in Trash for 30 days.
• Works everywhere in Chrome — on ordinary pages it floats beside the page; on Chrome's own pages it opens as a popup.
• Optional quick-open button — a small button in the corner of every page, off until you switch it on.
• Export anytime — a full backup you can restore, or one readable Markdown file for Obsidian, Notion, a document or an email.
• Make it yours — light, dark or system theme, six colour palettes, accent colours, and sans, serif or mono text.
• In your language — English, Hindi, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), German, French, Indonesian and Japanese.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• Your notes are stored only on this device, in Chrome's local extension storage.
• No account, no sign-up, no cloud, no sync, no ads, no analytics.
• The extension has no network access at all — it cannot send your notes anywhere.
• No access to your sites at install. Holdpad reads a page only when you click it or choose "Save selection"; the optional quick-open button asks for site access only if you turn it on.

WHO IT IS FOR

Students collecting quotes for an essay, researchers comparing sources, developers keeping commands and error messages, shoppers comparing prices, writers catching ideas, support agents keeping case details — anyone who needs a scratchpad next to the page they are working on.

HOW TO START

1. Pin Holdpad to your toolbar (puzzle-piece icon → pin).
2. Click the icon or press Alt+Shift+N on any page.
3. Type a note and press Ctrl/Cmd+Enter — or select text on the page, right-click, and choose "Save selection to Holdpad".
